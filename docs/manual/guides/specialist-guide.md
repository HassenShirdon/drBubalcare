# Specialist Guide

How to use the Dr. Bubal Care specialist (doctor) portal.

## Welcome

Welcome to Dr. Bubal Care as a specialist. You are the clinical decision-maker on this platform. AI handles coordination, pre-screening, and summaries — but you review the records, form the clinical impression, and sign the opinion.

**Portal URL:** `doctor.drbubalcare.com`

## Registration and Profile Setup

### Creating Your Account

1. Go to `doctor.drbubalcare.com`
2. Click **"Register as a Specialist"**
3. Provide:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Password
   - Medical specialty / subspecialties
   - Years of experience
   - Medical registration number
   - Location (for telehealth availability)
4. Submit your registration

### Credential Verification

After registration, you must verify your credentials:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Verification**
2. Upload supporting documents:
   - Medical license / registration certificate
   - Board certification (if applicable)
   - Hospital or clinic affiliation letter
3. Submit for review

Verification is typically completed within 24-48 hours. You'll receive an email notification when approved.

### Profile Setup

Complete your specialist profile from **Settings > Profile**:

- **Specialty** — Your primary specialty (e.g., Pathology, Radiology, Oncology)
- **Subspecialties** — Areas of focus (e.g., Dermatopathology, Neuroradiology)
- **Experience** — Years of experience (brief description)
- **Bio** — Professional biography for patient-facing display
- **Profile Image** — Professional photo
- **Location Type** — Telehealth, In-person, or both
- **Availability** — Your available hours and case capacity

## Case Review Workflow

### Receiving Cases

Cases are routed to you automatically by the AI based on your subspecialty match and availability. When a case is assigned:

1. You receive an email notification
2. The case appears in your **"My Cases"** dashboard with status `UNDER_REVIEW`
3. The AI pre-screening is attached to the case

### Reviewing a Case

1. **Open the case** from your dashboard
2. **Read the AI pre-screening** — This highlights key findings, differentials to consider, and any urgent flags. Review this first to understand what the AI surfaced.
3. **Review the clinical records** — Open each uploaded file (slides, scans, lab reports, clinical notes). Download files for detailed review.
4. **Review case metadata** — Service type, patient description, urgency flags

### The AI Pre-screening

The AI pre-screening is generated before you open the case. It includes:

- **Key findings** — Notable observations from the clinical records
- **Differentials to consider** — Potential diagnoses or conditions to rule in/out
- **Urgent flags** — Time-sensitive findings requiring immediate attention
- **Relevant history** — Any prior records or context

**Important:** The pre-screening is an AI-generated draft for your reference. It does not constitute a diagnosis. You must independently review all records and form your own clinical impression.

## Writing an Opinion

### Opinion Structure

Use the structured opinion editor to write your assessment. The editor provides these sections:

1. **Clinical Findings**
   - Describe what you observed in the records
   - Reference specific files or images
   - Note any technical details (margins, measurements, grades)

2. **Clinical Impression**
   - Your overall assessment
   - Differential diagnosis if applicable
   - Confidence level and rationale

3. **Recommended Next Steps**
   - Suggested actions (tests, referrals, treatment considerations)
   - Follow-up timeline
   - Any additional information needed from the patient

### Using the Editor

- The editor supports rich text formatting (headings, lists, bold, italic)
- Reference uploaded files by name
- Keep language precise and clinical
- Use standard medical terminology
- The editor auto-saves your draft every 30 seconds

### Opinion Best Practices

- **Be specific** — Reference exact file names and findings
- **Be clear about uncertainty** — State confidence levels and what additional information would help
- **Structure your opinion** — Use the three-section format consistently
- **Flag urgency** — If you identify a critical finding, note it prominently
- **Stay in scope** — Base your opinion only on the records provided

## Signing and Submitting

### Draft Review

- Your opinion is saved as `DRAFT` status while you work on it
- You can save and return to drafts at any time
- Review your complete opinion before signing

### Signing

1. Review your opinion one final time
2. Click **"Sign and Submit"**
3. Your digital signature is applied with a timestamp
4. Status changes from `DRAFT` → `SIGNED`
5. The patient is notified and can view the opinion
6. The plain-language summary is automatically generated

**Important:** Once signed, you cannot edit the opinion. If you need to make corrections, contact admin support.

### After Submission

- The opinion status becomes `DELIVERED` after the patient acknowledges receipt
- You can view your submitted opinions in **"My Opinions"**
- Patient follow-up questions may appear — you are not required to answer these (the AI handles them within scope)

## AI Feature Usage

### AI Pre-screening (Automatic)

- Generated automatically when a case is assigned to you
- Available in the case detail view
- Always labeled "AI-Generated Pre-screen — For Specialist Review Only"

### Plain-language Summary (Automatic)

- Generated automatically after you sign an opinion
- Translates your opinion into patient-friendly language
- Always labeled "AI-Generated Summary — Based on Specialist Opinion"
- You can preview the summary before the patient sees it

### Multi-opinion Synthesis (Automatic)

- Generated automatically when 2+ specialists sign opinions for the same case
- Shows areas of agreement and disagreement
- You can view the synthesis to see how your opinion compares to others

### Follow-up Q&A (AI-handled)

- Patient questions are answered by the AI within the scope of your opinion
- You are notified of follow-up questions but not required to respond
- If the AI cannot answer within scope, the patient is directed to book a consultation

### Trend Analysis (Patient-initiated)

- Patients with repeat lab tests can request trend analysis
- You are not directly involved in this feature

## Managing Availability

### Setting Available Hours

1. Go to **Settings > Availability**
2. Set your available hours per day
3. Set maximum cases per week
4. Save changes

### Case Capacity

- Set a weekly maximum for new cases
- When you reach capacity, new cases route to the next available specialist in your subspecialty
- You can adjust capacity at any time

### Temporary Unavailability

- Set vacation or unavailability dates in **Settings > Availability**
- Cases will not be routed to you during these periods
- Existing in-progress cases are not affected

## Common Tasks

### Reviewing DICOM Files

1. Open the case
2. Click on the DICOM file
3. Use the built-in DICOM viewer to scroll through slices
4. Adjust window/level for optimal viewing
5. Add findings to your opinion referencing specific slices

### Reviewing Histopathology Slides

1. Open the case
2. Click on the slide image
3. Use zoom and pan to examine regions of interest
4. Reference specific findings in your opinion

### Reviewing Lab Reports

1. Open the case
2. Click on the lab report PDF
3. Review values and reference ranges
4. Cross-reference with any trend analysis data
5. Note significant findings in your opinion

### Handling Urgent/Critical Findings

If you identify a critical finding requiring immediate attention:

1. Note the finding prominently in your opinion
2. Sign and submit immediately
3. The system will flag the case for urgent admin notification
4. Admin will arrange direct contact (phone/WhatsApp) with the patient

## Best Practices

| Practice | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Review AI pre-screen first | Saves time by highlighting what matters |
| Reference specific files | Helps patients and other specialists understand your reasoning |
| State uncertainty clearly | Honest assessment of confidence builds trust |
| Use standard terminology | Ensures consistency and machine readability |
| Flag critical findings early | Patient safety depends on timely notification |
| Keep opinions structured | Consistent format across all cases |
| Review the plain-language summary | Ensure it accurately reflects your opinion |

## FAQ

### How many cases can I review per week?

This depends on your availability settings. Set a comfortable weekly maximum and adjust as needed. The AI routing system respects your capacity limit.

### Can I decline a case?

Yes. If a case is routed to you but you feel it's outside your expertise, decline it through the case detail view. The system will re-route to the next available specialist.

### Can I communicate directly with the patient?

Direct communication is limited to the opinion content and structured follow-up system. For live discussion, patients can book a direct consultation appointment.

### What if I make an error after signing?

Contact admin support immediately. They can flag the opinion and arrange a correction or peer review. Do not attempt to modify signed opinions through the portal.

### How is my performance tracked?

Your response times, opinion quality, and case completion rates are tracked in the admin dashboard. This is for platform quality management, not for patient-facing display.

### Do I get paid per case?

Payment terms are defined in your specialist agreement with Dr. Bubal Care. Contact admin for payment-related questions.

### Can I see other specialists' opinions on the same case?

Yes, for panel reviews. When multiple specialists review the same case, you can see their opinions after all have signed. The multi-opinion synthesis provides an aggregated view.
