## Module 1: Getting Started

### Question 1: What does "AI surfaces; specialists decide" mean?

- A) AI provides the final diagnosis for every case
- B) AI coordinates your case and surfaces information, but a human specialist makes every clinical decision
- C) AI and specialists share equal responsibility for diagnoses
- D) You decide the diagnosis, and AI confirms it

**Correct answer: B**

Explanation: AI helps coordinate, pre-screen, and translate — but every clinical decision is made by a board-certified human specialist. This is the foundational principle of the Dr. Bubal Care platform.

---

- A) About 30 minutes
- B) About 5 minutes
- C) Under 2 minutes
- D) It requires admin approval first

Explanation: Registration takes under 2 minutes. You only need your name, email, country, and a password. No medical credentials are required.

- A) On the registration page

### Question 2: What happens when your case status reaches `AI_PRE_SCREENED`?

- A) A specialist has finished writing your opinion
- B) Your case has been closed and archived
- C) AI has analyzed your records and flagged key findings for the specialist
- D) You need to upload additional files

Explanation: At `AI_PRE_SCREENED`, AI has reviewed your records and prepared key findings for the incoming specialist. This follows the "AI surfaces; specialists decide" principle — AI prepares the clinical content; a human specialist makes the decisions.

### Question 3: What is the difference between single-expert and panel review?

- A) Single-expert is faster but less accurate; panel review is slower but always correct
- B) Single-expert gives one specialist opinion; panel review gives 2–4 independent opinions with an AI-generated synthesis
- C) Panel review is only available for urgent cases
- D) Single-expert does not include a written report

Explanation: Single-expert provides one opinion. Panel review provides 2–4 independent specialist opinions, and when all are received, the system generates a multi-opinion synthesis showing areas of agreement and disagreement.

- A) 25 MB
- B) 50 MB
- C) 100 MB

- A) It is shared with the specialist along with the clinical data

## Module 4: Understanding Reports

### Question 1: What are the three sections of a specialist opinion?

- A) Diagnosis, Treatment, Prognosis
- B) Clinical Findings, Clinical Impression, Recommended Next Steps
- C) Summary, Analysis, Recommendation
- D) Observations, Interpretation, Referral

**Correct answer: B**

Explanation: Every specialist opinion contains Clinical Findings (what was observed), Clinical Impression (the specialist's assessment), and Recommended Next Steps (actions to take next).

---

### Question 2: What is the purpose of the plain-language summary?

- A) To replace the specialist opinion with a simpler version
- B) To provide a second opinion from a different specialist
- C) To translate the specialist opinion into everyday language without medical jargon
- D) To summarize only the Recommended Next Steps section

**Correct answer: C**

Explanation: The plain-language summary is a patient-friendly translation of the specialist's signed opinion. It is not a separate opinion or a replacement — it helps you understand the clinical language.

---

### Question 3: What does the "Areas of Disagreement" section in a panel synthesis show?

- A) Where the AI disagrees with the specialist's opinion
- B) Where patient information was incomplete or missing
- C) Where specialists on the panel reached different conclusions or assessments
- D) Where the specialist opinion contradicts your local doctor's findings

**Correct answer: C**

Explanation: The Areas of Disagreement section highlights genuine differences in expert opinion. This reflects clinical complexity and helps you and your doctor make a more informed decision by understanding the range of expert perspectives.

## Module 5: Follow-up and Trends

### Question 1: What should you do if your follow-up question falls outside the scope of your opinion?

- A) Rephrase the question until the AI answers it
- B) Book a direct consultation with a specialist
- C) Email the specialist directly
- D) Wait and ask again later — the AI may change its response

**Correct answer: B**

Explanation: When a question falls outside the opinion's scope, the AI will redirect you to book a direct consultation for further clinical advice. The AI assistant answers only within the documented scope of the written opinion.

---

### Question 2: What does a red alert on a lab trend chart indicate?

- A) Your lab value is above the normal range
- B) Your lab value is below the normal range
- C) A statistically significant change has been detected that requires attention
- D) Your result has been uploaded incorrectly

**Correct answer: C**

Explanation: A red alert means the AI has detected a statistically significant change over time. It is an early-warning signal — not a diagnosis — that should be reviewed by your doctor.

---

### Question 3: Can the AI follow-up assistant recommend a treatment not mentioned in your specialist opinion?

- A) Yes, if the treatment is well-established
- B) Yes, but only for minor conditions
- C) No — it answers only within the documented scope of the written opinion
- D) No — it cannot answer any clinical questions

**Correct answer: C**

Explanation: The AI follow-up assistant operates strictly within the scope of the specialist's signed opinion. It cannot provide new diagnoses, recommend treatments not in the opinion, or override specialist recommendations.

## Module 6: Account and Privacy

### Question 1: Which of the following is NOT one of the six GDPR privacy rights?

- A) Right of Access
- B) Right to Rectification
- C) Right to a Free Consultation
- D) Right to Data Portability

**Correct answer: C**

Explanation: The six GDPR rights are: Right of Access (Article 15), Right to Rectification (Article 16), Right to Erasure (Article 17), Right to Restrict Processing (Article 18), Right to Data Portability (Article 20), and Right to Object (Article 21). There is no "Right to a Free Consultation" under GDPR.

---

### Question 2: What happens when you revoke consent for AI Pre-screening?

- A) Your account is deleted
- B) Your cases are no longer reviewed by specialists
- C) Your records are no longer pre-screened by AI before specialist review — but you still receive specialist opinions
- D) You lose access to the platform entirely

**Correct answer: C**

Explanation: Revoking AI Pre-screening consent stops AI from analyzing your records before specialist review. You can still create cases, upload records, and receive signed specialist opinions — the AI simply does not pre-screen.

---

### Question 3: How long does the platform take to execute a data deletion request?

- A) Immediately upon request
- B) Within 7 days
- C) Within 30 days (after a grace period to cancel the request)
- D) Within 72 hours

**Correct answer: C**

Explanation: After you request data deletion, there is a grace period of up to 30 days during which you can cancel. After the grace period, your data is permanently deleted (except anonymized audit logs retained for 7 years for regulatory compliance).
