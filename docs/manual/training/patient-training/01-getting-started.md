**Prerequisites:** None

## Welcome to Dr. Bubal Care

Dr. Bubal Care is an AI-assisted diagnostic coordination platform. It connects you with board-certified specialists around the world — no matter where you live. AI helps coordinate your case, but every clinical decision is made by a human specialist.

> **[PLACEHOLDER: screenshot]** — The Dr. Bubal Care homepage at `patient.drbubalcare.com`

You do not need to be a medical professional to use this platform. You only need your medical records and a few minutes to get started.

## Understanding the Platform

### The Core Principle

Every Dr. Bubal Care feature is built around one idea: **"AI surfaces; specialists decide."**

Here is what that means for you:

- **AI helps coordinate** your case — routing it to the right specialist, pre-screening your records, and translating reports into plain language.
- **A human specialist makes every clinical decision.** AI never diagnoses. Every written opinion is signed by a board-certified doctor.
- **You are in control.** You choose the service, upload your records, and decide whether to accept the opinion or raise a dispute.

### Your Patient Dashboard

After logging in, you land on your dashboard. Here is what you will find:

- **My Cases** — View and track all your active and past cases
- **Start a Case** — Begin a new request for a specialist opinion
- **Lab Trends** — View longitudinal tracking of repeat lab tests
- **Appointments** — Manage direct consultations with specialists
- **Settings** — Update your profile, password, and privacy preferences

## Creating Your Account

Registration takes under 2 minutes. You only need basic personal information — no medical credentials are required.

### Step 1: Go to the Portal

Open your browser and go to `patient.drbubalcare.com`.

### Step 2: Click Register

Click the **"Get Started"** or **"Register"** button on the homepage.

### Step 3: Fill in Your Details

Enter the following information:

- **Full name** — Your legal name as it appears on your ID
- **Email address** — This becomes your login and where notifications are sent
- **Country of residence** — Helps match you with available specialists
- **Password** — Minimum 8 characters

### Step 4: Verify Your Email

Check your inbox for a confirmation email. Click the verification link to activate your account. If you do not see the email, check your spam folder.

### Step 5: Complete Your Profile

After verification, you can add optional profile information. This is not required but helps specialists understand your context.

## Navigating Your Dashboard

Once registered, take a moment to familiarize yourself with the dashboard.

### Main Menu

The left-hand navigation gives you quick access to:

- **Dashboard** — Your home screen with case summaries
- **Cases** — Full list of your cases and their statuses
- **Lab Trends** — Charts tracking your lab results over time
- **Appointments** — Schedule and view direct consultations
- **Settings** — Account, privacy, and notification preferences

### Notifications

You receive email notifications at every important stage:

- When a specialist begins reviewing your case
- When your opinion is ready
- When there are status changes on your case

You can configure notification preferences from **Settings > Notifications**.

### Profile Settings

Access your settings from the dashboard menu:

- **Profile** — Update your name, email, and country
- **Password** — Change your password at any time
- **Privacy** — Manage consent for AI processing and data usage
- **Notifications** — Choose which emails you receive
- **Delete Account** — Request full data deletion under GDPR Article 17

## Practice Scenario

**Scenario:** You are a new patient. You have a set of lab results from your local doctor that you want interpreted by a specialist, but you are not sure what the numbers mean.

**Your task:**
1. Go to `patient.drbubalcare.com` and register a new account using your real name and email
2. Verify your email address via the confirmation link
3. Log in and explore the dashboard — click through **My Cases**, **Lab Trends**, and **Settings**
4. Update your notification preferences to receive email alerts for case status changes

**Expected outcome:** You have a verified account, you can log in successfully, and you are familiar with the main dashboard navigation.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | What to Do Instead |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Using a disposable email address | You will miss important case notifications and cannot verify your account | Use a permanent email address you check regularly |
| Skipping email verification | Your account remains inactive and you cannot start cases | Click the verification link immediately after registering |
| Ignoring notification settings | You may miss when your specialist opinion is ready | Review and enable email notifications in **Settings > Notifications** |
| Sharing your password | Compromises your medical data security | Use a unique, strong password and never share it |
