## What Is a Case?

A case is your request for a specialist opinion on your medical records. When you create a case, you are asking a board-certified specialist to review your slides, scans, lab results, or clinical notes and provide a written opinion.

Every case follows a clear workflow:

1. You describe your situation and upload your records
2. AI pre-screens your records and flags key findings
3. A matched specialist reviews your case
4. You receive a written opinion — a clinical findings section, an impression, and recommended next steps

The entire process follows the platform's core principle: **"AI surfaces; specialists decide."** AI helps coordinate and prepare your case, but a human specialist makes every clinical decision.

## Step 1: Choose a Service Type

Dr. Bubal Care offers four service types. Choose the one that matches your situation.

| Service Type | What You Get | When to Use It |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Specialist Opinion** | Written opinion from a board-certified specialist within 24–48 hours | You have slides, scans, or reports that need expert review |
| **Result Interpretation** | Plain-language explanation of existing results from a qualified specialist | You have lab results but need help understanding what they mean |
| **Direct Consultation** | Live video consultation with a specialist in real time | You need to discuss your case directly and ask questions |
| **Trend Analysis** | Longitudinal tracking of lab results with AI-flagged significant changes | You have repeat lab tests that need to be tracked over time |

### How to Select

1. Log in to `patient.drbubalcare.com`
2. Click **"Start a Case"** on your dashboard
3. Choose the service type that fits your situation
4. You will see the exact price before confirming

## Step 2: Describe Your Case

Once you select a service type, you need to describe your situation. This helps the platform match you with the right specialist.

### What to Include

- **Body system or specialty area** — Select the relevant area (e.g., oncology, cardiology, neurology)
- **Brief description** — A short summary of your situation in plain language
- **Urgency level** — Choose from:
  - **Routine** — Standard turnaround is fine
  - **Soon** — You need the opinion within a few days
  - **Urgent** — There is a time-sensitive concern

### Tips for Writing a Good Description

- Keep it simple and factual
- Mention when your symptoms started or when tests were taken
- Note any relevant medical history
- Avoid including personally identifying information in the description — your identity is protected separately

**Example:**

> "I had a chest X-ray done on January 15, 2026, after persistent coughing for three weeks. My local doctor noticed something unusual in the right lower lobe and recommended specialist review."

## Step 4: Choose Single-Expert or Panel Review

Before submitting your case, you choose how many specialists review your records.

### Single-Expert Review

- One board-certified specialist reviews your case
- Faster turnaround
- Lower cost
- Best for straightforward cases

### Panel Review

- 2–4 specialists independently review your case
- When all opinions are received, the system generates a **multi-opinion synthesis** that includes:
  - **Areas of agreement** — Where all specialists agree
  - **Areas of disagreement** — Where opinions differ
  - **Consensus summary** — An overall synthesis of the panel
- Best for complex or uncertain cases where multiple perspectives add value

## Case Status Flow

After you submit your case and confirm payment, your case moves through these stages:

```
OPEN → AI_PRE_SCREENED → UNDER_REVIEW → OPINION_READY → CLOSED
```

| Status | What It Means | What Happens Next |
|--------|--------------|-------------------|
| `OPEN` | Your case has been submitted and payment confirmed | AI begins pre-screening your records |
| `AI_PRE_SCREENED` | AI has analyzed your records and flagged key findings for the specialist | A specialist is matched and assigned to your case |
| `UNDER_REVIEW` | A specialist is reviewing your records and the AI pre-screen | Wait for the opinion — target is 24–48 hours |
| `OPINION_READY` | Your specialist's opinion is available in your portal | Log in to read your report and plain-language summary |
| `CLOSED` | The case is complete — opinion delivered and acknowledged | You can ask follow-up questions or start a new case |

You also receive email notifications at each status change so you never have to guess.

## Practice Scenario

**Scenario:** You have a set of blood test results from your local clinic. Your doctor mentioned that some values were slightly elevated but did not explain what they mean. You want a specialist to interpret the results in plain language.

**Your task:**
1. Log in to `patient.drbubalcare.com` and click **"Start a Case"**
2. Select **Result Interpretation** as your service type
3. Select **Internal Medicine** as the specialty area
4. Write a brief description: "Blood test results from January 2026 showing elevated liver enzymes. Local doctor said values were slightly high but did not explain further."
5. Set urgency to **Routine**
6. Choose **Single-Expert Review**
7. Upload your lab results PDF with the file name "Liver Panel - Jan 2026"
8. Confirm payment and submit the case

**Expected outcome:** Your case is created with status `OPEN`, you receive a confirmation email, and the case appears in your dashboard under **My Cases**.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | What to Do Instead |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Choosing the wrong service type | You may pay for a service that does not match your needs | Read the service descriptions carefully and choose the one that fits — **Result Interpretation** for understanding existing results, **Specialist Opinion** for new review |
| Uploading files without descriptive names | The specialist cannot easily identify which file is which | Name files clearly, e.g., "MRI Brain - Feb 2026" instead of "scan1.dcm" |
| Selecting Panel Review for a simple question | You pay more for multiple opinions when one specialist is sufficient | Use **Single-Expert Review** for straightforward cases; save **Panel Review** for complex or uncertain situations |
| Including personal details in the case description | Your identity should only be in your account profile, not in clinical descriptions | Keep descriptions focused on medical facts — dates, symptoms, test results |

   - **Correct answer:** C — At `AI_PRE_SCREENED`, AI has reviewed your records and prepared key findings for the incoming specialist. This follows the "AI surfaces; specialists decide" principle.

3. **What is the difference between single-expert and panel review?**
   - A) Single-expert is faster but less accurate; panel review is slower but always correct
   - B) Single-expert gives one specialist opinion; panel review gives 2–4 independent opinions with an AI-generated synthesis
   - C) Panel review is only available for urgent cases
   - D) Single-expert does not include a written report
   - **Correct answer:** B — Single-expert provides one opinion. Panel review provides 2–4 independent specialist opinions, and when all are received, the system generates a multi-opinion synthesis showing areas of agreement and disagreement.
