## Profile Management

Your account settings are accessible from the **Settings** menu on your dashboard. Here is what you can manage:

| Setting | What You Can Change | How to Access |
|---------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Profile** | Update your name, email, and country of residence | **Settings > Profile** |
| **Password** | Change your password at any time | **Settings > Password** |
| **Notifications** | Choose which email alerts you receive (case status, opinion ready, lab alerts) | **Settings > Notifications** |
| **Privacy** | Manage consent for AI processing and request data deletion | **Settings > Privacy** |

### Updating Your Profile

1. Go to **Settings > Profile**
2. Edit your name, email, or country
3. Click **"Save Changes"**
4. If you change your email, you will need to re-verify via a confirmation link sent to the new address

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to **Settings > Password**
2. Enter your current password
3. Enter your new password (minimum 8 characters)
4. Confirm your new password
5. Click **"Update Password"**

You will receive a confirmation email when your password is changed. If you did not make this change, contact support immediately through the portal.

### Notification Preferences

You can choose which email notifications you receive. Available options include:

- **Case status changes** — When your case moves from `OPEN` to `AI_PRE_SCREENED` to `UNDER_REVIEW` to `OPINION_READY`
- **Opinion ready** — When a specialist has signed and delivered your opinion
- **Lab trend alerts** — When a yellow or red alert is detected on your tracked lab results
- **Follow-up responses** — When the AI assistant responds to your follow-up question
- **Appointment reminders** — Upcoming consultation reminders

Recommended: Keep at least **case status changes** and **opinion ready** notifications enabled so you never miss an important update.

## Data Deletion

Under **GDPR Article 17** (the right to erasure), you can request the permanent deletion of all your personal data from the platform. This is sometimes called the "right to be forgotten."

### How to Request Data Deletion

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy**
2. Click **"Request Data Deletion"**
3. Confirm your request — you will be asked to verify this is your intention
4. Your deletion request is logged and queued

### What Happens After You Request Deletion

| Step | Timeframe | What Happens |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Request received** | Immediately | Your request is logged and confirmed via email |
| **Grace period** | Up to 30 days | You have a window to cancel the request if you change your mind |
| **Deletion executed** | After grace period | Your account, cases, files, and personal data are permanently deleted |
| **Audit logs retained** | 7 years | Anonymized audit logs are retained for regulatory compliance (GDPR/HIPAA) |
| **Consent records retained** | 7 years | Anonymized consent records are retained as proof of compliance |

### What Gets Deleted

- Your account profile (name, email, country)
- All cases and uploaded files
- Specialist opinions (anonymized — the specialist's work is retained but your identity is removed)
- Lab results and trend data
- Follow-up questions and AI responses
- All personal identifiers

### What Is Retained

- **Audit logs** — Anonymized, retained for 7 years for regulatory compliance
- **Consent records** — Anonymized, retained for 7 years as proof of lawful consent

You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is complete.

## Consent Management

When you use AI-powered features on Dr. Bubal Care, the platform requires your explicit consent to process your clinical data through AI systems. You can manage these consents from **Settings > Privacy**.

### Consent Types

| Consent Type | What It Covers | Required for AI Features? |
|-------------|----------------|--------------------------|
| **AI Pre-screening** | Allows AI to analyze your clinical records and flag key findings before a specialist opens your case | Yes — without this, AI pre-screening is not performed |
| **Plain-language Summary** | Allows AI to translate the specialist's opinion into everyday language | Yes — without this, the plain-language summary is not generated |
| **Trend Analysis** | Allows AI to track your lab results over time and flag statistically significant changes | Yes — without this, trend tracking is not active |

All three consent types are **revocable**. You can change your mind at any time.

### How to Revoke Consent

1. Go to **Settings > Privacy**
2. Find the consent type you want to revoke (e.g., **AI Pre-screening**)
3. Click **"Revoke Consent"**
4. Confirm the revocation

Revoking consent does **not** affect your ability to receive specialist opinions. You can still create cases, upload records, and receive signed specialist opinions without AI consent. The AI features simply will not be performed for your account.

### What Changes When You Revoke Consent

| Consent Revoked | Effect on Your Account |
|----------------|----------------------|
| **AI Pre-screening** | Your records are no longer pre-screened by AI before specialist review — the specialist receives your records without AI-flagged findings |
| **Plain-language Summary** | The specialist opinion is delivered without a plain-language translation — you will need to read the clinical language or ask your local doctor to explain |
| **Trend Analysis** | Your lab results are no longer tracked longitudinally — you will not receive yellow or red trend alerts |

If you revoke a consent and later want to re-enable the feature, you can return to **Settings > Privacy** and grant consent again.

## Your Privacy Rights

As a patient on Dr. Bubal Care, you have six rights under the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**. These rights ensure you are always in control of your personal data.

| Right | What It Means | How to Exercise It |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Right of Access** (Article 15) | You can request a copy of all personal data the platform holds about you — including your profile, cases, opinions, AI outputs, and consent records | Go to **Settings > Privacy > Request Data Export** |
| **Right to Rectification** (Article 16) | You can request correction of inaccurate personal data — such as a wrong name or email address | Edit directly in **Settings > Profile**, or contact support for clinical records |
| **Right to Erasure** (Article 17) | You can request permanent deletion of all your personal data | Go to **Settings > Privacy > Request Data Deletion** |
| **Right to Restrict Processing** (Article 18) | You can request that the platform limit how your data is processed — for example, pausing AI processing while a dispute is being reviewed | Contact support through the portal with your specific restriction request |
| **Right to Data Portability** (Article 20) | You can receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (JSON, PDF, or CSV) | Go to **Settings > Privacy > Request Data Export** — choose JSON, PDF, or CSV format |
| **Right to Object** (Article 21) | You can object to the processing of your data for specific purposes, including direct marketing | Contact support through the portal with your objection |

### Data Export Formats

When you request a data export under the Right of Access or Right to Data Portability, you can choose the format:

| Format | What It Contains |
|--------|-----------------|
| **JSON** | Full data export — profile, cases, records, opinions, AI outputs, consent records |
| **PDF** | Specialist opinions and plain-language summaries — easy to share with your local doctor |
| **CSV** | Audit log of personal data access — who accessed your data and when |

Export links expire after 24 hours for security reasons.

## Practice Scenario

**Scenario:** You have been using Dr. Bubal Care for several months. You have received two specialist opinions and have been tracking your lab trends. You now want to review your privacy settings, understand what data the platform holds about you, and update your consent preferences.

**Your task:**
1. Log in to `patient.drbubalcare.com`
2. Go to **Settings > Profile** and review your current information — update your country if it has changed
3. Go to **Settings > Privacy** and review your current consent settings
4. Revoke consent for **Trend Analysis** — confirm you understand this will stop tracking your lab results
5. Request a **Data Export** in PDF format — confirm you receive an email with the download link
6. Review the exported data to see what the platform holds about you
7. Go back to **Settings > Privacy** and re-enable **Trend Analysis** consent
8. Review the six GDPR rights listed in this module and note which ones you might use in the future

**Expected outcome:** You have updated your profile, managed your consent preferences, exported your data, and understand your GDPR privacy rights.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | What to Do Instead |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Revoking all AI consent without understanding the effect | Revoking AI Pre-screening and Plain-language Summary consent means you lose these helpful features — you can still receive specialist opinions, but without AI assistance | Review what each consent type controls before revoking — you can revoke and re-enable at any time |
| Ignoring the data export feature | You have a right to know what data the platform holds about you — regular exports help you stay informed | Request a data export periodically to review what personal data is stored |
| Sharing your account with a family member | Each patient account should have one owner — shared accounts compromise data security and consent records | Each family member should register their own account |
| Requesting data deletion without backing up your records | Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered (except anonymized audit logs) | Export your data as PDF or JSON before requesting deletion, if you want to keep a copy |
| Assuming GDPR rights do not apply to you because you are outside the EU | Dr. Bubal Care applies GDPR standards to all patients regardless of location | All six GDPR rights are available to every patient on the platform |

## Knowledge Check

1. **Which of the following is NOT one of the six GDPR privacy rights?**
   - A) Right of Access
   - B) Right to Rectification
   - C) Right to a Free Consultation
   - D) Right to Data Portability
   - **Correct answer:** C — The six GDPR rights are: Right of Access, Right to Rectification, Right to Erasure, Right to Restrict Processing, Right to Data Portability, and Right to Object. There is no "Right to a Free Consultation" under GDPR.

2. **What happens when you revoke consent for AI Pre-screening?**
   - A) Your account is deleted
   - B) Your cases are no longer reviewed by specialists
   - C) Your records are no longer pre-screened by AI before specialist review — but you still receive specialist opinions
   - D) You lose access to the platform entirely
   - **Correct answer:** C — Revoking AI Pre-screening consent stops AI from analyzing your records before specialist review. You can still create cases, upload records, and receive signed specialist opinions — the AI simply does not pre-screen.

3. **How long does the platform take to execute a data deletion request?**
   - A) Immediately upon request
   - B) Within 7 days
   - C) Within 30 days (after a grace period to cancel the request)
   - D) Within 72 hours
   - **Correct answer:** C — After you request data deletion, there is a grace period of up to 30 days during which you can cancel. After the grace period, your data is permanently deleted (except anonymized audit logs retained for 7 years for regulatory compliance).
