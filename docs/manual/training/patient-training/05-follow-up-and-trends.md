## Follow-up Q&A

After your specialist opinion is delivered, you may have questions about the findings, impression, or recommended next steps. The platform provides an **AI follow-up assistant** that answers your questions — within the scope of the written opinion.

### How to Ask a Follow-up Question

1. Open your completed case from **My Cases** on your dashboard
2. Click **"Ask a Follow-up Question"**
3. Type your question in plain language
4. The AI assistant responds within the documented scope of your opinion

The AI assistant draws only from your specialist's signed opinion and the clinical records you uploaded. It does not access external medical databases or offer independent medical advice.

### Example Questions

Not all questions are equally useful for the follow-up assistant. Here are examples of good and poor questions.

| Good Question | Why It Works |
|---------------|-------------|
| "What does the recommended CT scan involve?" | The opinion recommends a CT scan — the AI can explain this within scope |
| "How soon should I schedule the pulmonology referral?" | The opinion recommends a pulmonology referral — the AI can clarify timing |
| "What does 'well-defined margins' mean in my findings?" | This refers directly to the specialist's Clinical Findings section |
| "Can you explain the three differential diagnoses in plain language?" | The opinion lists differentials — the AI can translate them into everyday terms |

| Poor Question | Why It Falls Outside Scope |
|---------------|---------------------------|
| "What treatment should I start immediately?" | The specialist opinion does not prescribe treatment — the AI cannot recommend treatments not in the opinion |
| "Is the nodule definitely cancerous?" | The AI cannot provide a definitive diagnosis — only the specialist's assessment of probability |
| "Should I get a second opinion from a different specialist?" | This is a decision outside the scope of the written opinion |
| "Can you prescribe medication for my cough?" | The AI does not prescribe or recommend medications — only the specialist can advise on clinical decisions |

### Scope Limits

The AI follow-up assistant operates within strict boundaries:

- **Questions must relate to the existing opinion.** If your question falls outside the opinion's scope, the AI will let you know and suggest booking a direct consultation.
- **The AI cannot provide new diagnoses.** It can explain what the specialist wrote, but it cannot add new clinical interpretations.
- **The AI cannot recommend treatments not in the opinion.** If the specialist recommended a CT scan, the AI can explain that recommendation — but it cannot suggest alternative treatments.
- **The AI cannot override specialist recommendations.** The specialist's signed opinion is the authoritative clinical document.

If the AI determines your question is outside scope, it will respond with a message like:

> "This question goes beyond the scope of your current specialist opinion. For further clinical advice, I recommend booking a direct consultation with a specialist."

## Viewing Lab Trends

If you have repeat lab tests — for example, blood work taken at different points in time — the platform tracks them longitudinally in the **Lab Trends** section of your dashboard.

### How to Access Lab Trends

1. Log in to `patient.drbubalcare.com`
2. Click **"Lab Trends"** in the left-hand navigation
3. You will see a list of tracked lab tests and their trend charts
4. Click on any test name to view its detailed trend chart

> **[PLACEHOLDER: screenshot]** — The Lab Trends dashboard showing tracked tests and trend charts

### Reading Trend Charts

Each trend chart shows your lab values over time, with reference ranges indicated. The AI highlights statistically significant changes using a color-coded alert system.

| Alert Color | What It Means | What to Do |
|-------------|--------------|------------|
| **Green** | Your value is within the normal reference range | No action needed — your results are stable and normal |
| **Yellow** | Your value is slightly outside the normal range | Review the result — discuss with your local doctor if this is a new pattern |
| **Red** | A statistically significant change has been detected | This requires attention — contact your local doctor or book a consultation |

When you see a **red** alert, the system also notifies your care team. This is not a diagnosis — it is an early-warning signal that something has changed in a statistically meaningful way and should be reviewed.

### Interpreting Your Trends

Trend analysis tracks changes over time, not individual values. A single test result within the normal range may not trigger any alert, but a consistent upward or downward trend across multiple tests may trigger a **yellow** or **red** alert even if individual values appear normal.

**Example:**

- Your hemoglobin was 13.2 g/dL in January and 12.1 g/dL in June — both within the normal range (12.0–16.0 g/dL for women)
- The AI detects a statistically significant downward trend and flags a **yellow** alert
- This does not mean you are anaemic — it means the rate of change warrants attention

## Booking Direct Consultations

When your question falls outside the scope of follow-up Q&A, or when you prefer a live conversation with a specialist, you can book a **Direct Consultation**.

### When to Book a Consultation

- Your follow-up question was flagged as outside the opinion's scope
- You want to discuss treatment options (the AI cannot recommend treatments)
- You want a real-time conversation rather than written responses
- You have new symptoms or test results not covered by the current opinion
- You want a specialist to review new records that have not been submitted as a case

### How to Book

1. Go to **"Appointments"** in your dashboard
2. Browse available specialists and filter by specialty
3. Select a specialist and view their available time slots
4. Choose a date and time that works for you
5. Confirm and pay — you will see the price before confirming
6. Receive a calendar invitation and email reminders

Appointment statuses: `SCHEDULED` → `CONFIRMED` → `IN_PROGRESS` → `COMPLETED` (or `CANCELLED`)

## Practice Scenario

**Scenario:** You received a specialist opinion about your blood test results last week. The opinion recommended repeat lab tests in 3 months and mentioned a possible thyroid issue. You have a few questions and also want to track your results over time.

**Your task:**
1. Log in to `patient.drbubalcare.com` and open your completed case
2. Click **"Ask a Follow-up Question"**
3. Ask: "What does the opinion mean by 'possible thyroid issue' and what tests should I ask my local doctor about?"
4. Read the AI response — confirm it stays within the scope of your opinion
5. Now ask: "Should I start medication immediately?" — note how the AI responds when a question falls outside scope
6. Navigate to **Lab Trends** in your dashboard
7. Review your tracked lab values — identify any yellow or red alerts
8. If you have questions about a red alert, click on the data point to see the specific result and date

**Expected outcome:** You can ask follow-up questions that stay within scope, understand when the AI redirects you to a consultation, and interpret your lab trend alerts correctly.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong | What to Do Instead |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| Asking the AI for treatment recommendations | The AI follow-up assistant answers only within the scope of the written opinion — it cannot recommend treatments not documented by the specialist | Ask questions that relate to the opinion's findings or recommendations, or book a direct consultation for treatment discussions |
| Ignoring red trend alerts | A red alert indicates a statistically significant change that may require clinical attention | Review the alert, check the specific result and date, and contact your local doctor or book a consultation |
| Assuming a single normal value means no concern | Trend analysis tracks changes over time — a consistent direction of change can trigger an alert even if individual values are within range | Review your full trend chart and discuss patterns with your doctor |
| Using follow-up Q&A for new records you have not uploaded | The AI answers based on the existing opinion and uploaded records — it cannot assess records it has not seen | Upload new records by starting a new case or booking a direct consultation |

## Knowledge Check

1. **What should you do if your follow-up question falls outside the scope of your opinion?**
   - A) Rephrase the question until the AI answers it
   - B) Book a direct consultation with a specialist
   - C) Email the specialist directly
   - D) Wait and ask again later — the AI may change its response
   - **Correct answer:** B — When a question falls outside the opinion's scope, the AI will redirect you to book a direct consultation for further clinical advice.

2. **What does a red alert on a lab trend chart indicate?**
   - A) Your lab value is above the normal range
   - B) Your lab value is below the normal range
   - C) A statistically significant change has been detected that requires attention
   - D) Your result has been uploaded incorrectly
   - **Correct answer:** C — A red alert means the AI has detected a statistically significant change over time. It is an early-warning signal — not a diagnosis — that should be reviewed by your doctor.

3. **Can the AI follow-up assistant recommend a treatment not mentioned in your specialist opinion?**
   - A) Yes, if the treatment is well-established
   - B) Yes, but only for minor conditions
   - C) No — it answers only within the documented scope of the written opinion
   - D) No — it cannot answer any clinical questions
   - **Correct answer:** C — The AI follow-up assistant operates strictly within the scope of the specialist's signed opinion. It cannot provide new diagnoses, recommend treatments not in the opinion, or override specialist recommendations.

## Summary

In this module, you learned:

- The **AI follow-up assistant** answers questions about your specialist opinion within the documented scope of the written report
- Good follow-up questions reference specific findings, recommendations, or terms from the opinion
- Questions about new treatments, diagnoses, or clinical decisions outside the opinion fall outside scope — you will be directed to book a consultation
- The **Lab Trends** section tracks your repeat lab tests over time with color-coded alerts: **green** (normal), **yellow** (slightly outside range), **red** (statistically significant change)
- A **red alert** is not a diagnosis — it is an early-warning signal that should be reviewed by your doctor
- You can book a **Direct Consultation** for live conversation with a specialist when written follow-up is not enough
